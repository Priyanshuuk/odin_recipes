Burger

Original recipe (1X) yields 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 pound meeat
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 slices cheddar cheese (optional)
  • Lettuce, tomato, pickles, and condiments (optional)

Directions

  1. Preheat a grill or stovetop pan to medium-high heat.
  2. In a bowl, mix ground meat with salt and pepper. Shape the mixture into 4 patties.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan or on the grill. Cook the patties for 4-5 minutes on each side, or until they reach your desired level of doneness.
  4. In the last minute of cooking, place a slice of cheddar cheese on each patty (if using) and cover to melt.
  5. Toast the hamburger buns on the grill or in a toaster.
  6. Assemble the burger by placing the patty on the bottom bun and adding your favorite toppings like lettuce, tomato, and pickles.
  7. Top with the other half of the bun and serve immediately.
