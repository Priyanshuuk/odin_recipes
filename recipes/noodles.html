Noodles

Original recipe (1X) yields 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces noodles (any variety like spaghetti, ramen, etc.)
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup vegetables (carrots, bell peppers, peas, etc.)
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon chili sauce or hot sauce (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Green onions for garnish (optional)

Directions

  1. Boil water in a large pot and cook noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the vegetables and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes until they are tender-crisp.
  4. Stir in the soy sauce and sesame oil (if using), then add the cooked noodles to the pan.
  5. Toss everything together, ensuring the noodles are coated in the sauce and vegetables are evenly distributed.
  6. Season with salt, pepper, and chili sauce (if using) to taste.
  7. Serve hot, garnished with green onions if desired.
